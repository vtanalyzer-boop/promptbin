## Why web stories help

Slides force each idea to be short. That is a useful habit for every ChatGPT prompt for photo editing: one look, one light, one crop and one identity lock. When you write your own ChatGPT photo editing prompts, imagine each as a single slide.

## What a story can and can't show you

A story is a preview, not a lesson. In six slides you can see the look, the light and one tip, but you can't see how ChatGPT handled someone else's photo, and that matters. The same ChatGPT AI photo editing prompts can give a soft, flattering result on one picture and a flat one on another.

So treat the slide as a menu. It tells you what's on offer. The finished edit still depends on your photo, your plan and the version of ChatGPT's image editing you're using, and that varies from week to week.

## Read the style words on each slide

Every slide boils a look down to a handful of phrases. Learn to spot them and you'll start writing your own ChatGPT prompt for photo editing without a template.

- **Light words:** golden hour, window light, direct flash, neon glow.
- **Texture words:** film grain, faded colors, painted brush strokes, glossy plastic.
- **Frame words:** vertical 4:5, close crop, white border, three panels.
- **Lock words:** keep the face, outfit and pose unchanged.

If a slide has all four kinds, the prompt behind it is probably complete. If one is missing, usually the lock or the frame, that's the first thing to add yourself.

## A worked example: slide to finished edit

Say the trending Instagram story shows a 90s flash look and you want it on a photo of your friends at a birthday dinner. Here's what you'd do.

1. Note the style words from the slide: direct flash, slight grain, warm faded colors, date-stamp corner.
2. Open the 90s disposable camera card in the [prompt library](/) and copy it.
3. Paste it into ChatGPT with your photo, then add your own lock line at the end.

> Edit this photo to look like a 90s disposable camera snapshot. Harsh direct flash, no red-eye, soft grain, warm faded colors and a small orange date stamp in the bottom corner. Keep every face, outfit and pose exactly the same. Vertical 4:5.

If the date stamp comes out garbled, don't regenerate everything. Ask: "Keep the image the same, but make the date stamp read 12 10 26 in small orange digits." One fix, one message.

## When a story prompt doesn't work on your photo

It happens. Here are the usual causes and what to do about each.

- **The face changed:** add the identity lock again and put it first, not last.
- **The look is too strong:** write "subtle" or "light touch" next to the style word, for example "subtle film grain".
- **Your photo is dark:** say "brighten the original slightly, then apply the look". Flash and golden hour styles need something to work with.
- **It ignored the crop:** repeat it in the last sentence, in plain words like "vertical, 4:5".

If three attempts don't get you close, switch photos. Some pictures just fight a style, and that's not your prompt's fault.
