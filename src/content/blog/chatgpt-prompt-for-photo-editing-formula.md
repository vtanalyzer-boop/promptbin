Prompts feel random until you notice that the good ones share a structure. This article gives you a six-part formula you can reuse for almost any edit, plus templates for portraits, backgrounds and cleanup. Once you have the formula, writing a ChatGPT prompt for photo editing takes about thirty seconds.

## The six-part formula

1. **Action.** What are you asking for? Edit, redraw, replace, remove, convert.
2. **Subject.** Which part of the photo? "My photo", "the couple", "only the background".
3. **Style.** One look: cinematic, hand-painted, editorial, film.
4. **Light and color.** Golden hour, soft studio, neon, muted pastels.
5. **Protection.** What must not change: face, outfit, pose, edges of hair.
6. **Output.** Crop and size: 4:5, 9:16, square.

A ChatGPT prompt for photo editing that contains all six parts is long enough to be precise and short enough to read at a glance. The ChatGPT AI photo editing prompts in our library all follow it.

## A worked example: from vague to six parts

Say you type: "make my photo look nice and professional." ChatGPT will guess, and the guess usually means heavy smoothing and a random backdrop. Here is how the same request grows when you apply the formula one part at a time.

1. Action and subject: "Edit this photo of me into a portrait."
2. Style: "Editorial, like a magazine feature."
3. Light and color: "Soft window light from the left, muted warm tones."
4. Protection: "Keep my face, hairstyle and glasses exactly the same."
5. Output: "Vertical 4:5."

Joined together, it reads like this:

> Edit this photo of me into an editorial portrait. Soft window light from the left, muted warm tones, natural skin texture. Keep my face, hairstyle and glasses exactly the same. Vertical 4:5.

That is 35 words, and every one of them is doing a job. Nothing in it is clever. It is just specific.

## When the result goes wrong, change the matching part

Most bad results trace back to one missing or vague part. Use this as a quick lookup before you rewrite anything.

- **The face looks different.** Part 5 is too weak. Name the features: "keep my face shape, eyes, nose and smile unchanged."
- **The skin looks plastic.** You probably asked for "flawless" or "perfect". Swap in "natural skin texture, no heavy smoothing."
- **The style is muddy or mixed.** You asked for two or three looks at once. Pick one and drop the rest.
- **It cropped my head.** Part 6 was missing. Add the ratio and "leave space above the head."
- **It changed the clothes when you only wanted a new background.** Part 2 was too loose. Write "only the background" and list what stays.

If two fixes are needed, make them one at a time. Otherwise you will never know which one worked.

## How long should the prompt be?

Short prompts leave too much to chance, and very long ones start to contradict themselves. For most edits, somewhere between 30 and 80 words is a comfortable range. If you are past 100 words, look for repeated ideas and cut the weaker copy.

Results still vary by photo, ChatGPT version, plan and region, so treat any template as a strong first draft. It won't work perfectly every time, and that is normal.
